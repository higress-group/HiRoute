# Frozen parent editorial assessment

The parent reads code-named draft/final pairs, without model/effort labels or
reviewer verdicts, and records each score before unblinding. One evaluator is a
limitation; report observable findings and excerpts, not an invented inter-rater
agreement. Read final reviews only after independent article scores are saved.

Each dimension is scored 0–4: 0 absent or seriously misleading; 1 weak with major
defects; 2 adequate but uneven; 3 strong with small defects; 4 unusually effective
with no material defect in this dimension. Weighted total is sum(weight × score/4).

| Dimension | Weight | What counts |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Factual fidelity and epistemic care | 30 | Correct technical scope, uncertainty, source attribution, no invented reporting |
| Explanatory depth | 20 | Explains the difference between results and understanding and the mechanisms affecting training/incentives |
| Argument and structure | 15 | Coherent thesis, earned transitions, useful progression and ending |
| Concrete evidence and synthesis | 15 | Selects and connects specific evidence; distinguishes facts, values and predictions |
| Fairness and counterargument | 10 | Takes a serious alternative view and tradeoffs on their merits |
| Prose and reader engagement | 10 | Precise, readable, varied language; apt original images rather than stock rhetoric |

## Gates and defects

An article is editorially acceptable for this experiment if its total is at least
75/100, factual fidelity at least 3/4, and it has no critical factual violation.
These thresholds are pragmatic, predeclared editorial judgments, not calibrated
population measurements. Report raw scores even when a gate fails.

Critical violations include invented interviews/firsthand attendance/direct
quotes; treating the unforced Navier–Stokes case as solved by S1; asserting that
100 company-claimed results were independently verified here; presenting the
arXiv policy as an AI ban or a limit applied to all coauthors; and attributing
control over internal progress to the advisory group. Other materially false
claims can also be critical, with an explicit explanation. Honest omission of a
detail is not a false claim. Minor imprecision is scored proportionately.

Length outside 1,080–1,760 words or missing source markers is a delivery defect,
reported separately, not an automatic factual-zero. Similarity to the human
original's title, imagery or ordering is not a scoring target.

## Review quality and revision effect

After scoring articles, annotate review findings as valid, unsupported, or
stylistic preference. Count substantive errors caught, invented objections,
useful structural advice, accepted/rejected suggestions, and new revision errors.
Compute each dimension's draft-to-final delta and the number of gates gained/lost.
Do not reward criticism volume or a reviewer's self-assigned verdict.

After freezing the blind scores, open the mapping and report each arm/effort's
two results, paired differences and uncertainty. Separately compare the human
reference's narrative/explanatory strengths and weaknesses against representative
AI excerpts. Human original has a different length, interviews and editorial
process; do not manufacture a controlled human-vs-model win rate. A future article
about HiRoute must show all registered arms and disclose this narrow sample.
